# 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

make.fun bounty entry: [Sum up 19 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

A newspaper front page (`entry.png`, 683×1024 PNG) summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six stories:

1. **Trump, Xi & Putin meet in Shenzhen** — First-ever three-way summit at APEC; 90-minute talks end in a "peace declaration" on nuclear risks.
2. **World court: Myanmar committed genocide** — ICJ rules 9–6 over the 2017 Rohingya crackdown, a first for the UN court; Myanmar must pay compensation.
3. **Anthropic's record IPO debuts** — ANT opens at $111.50 on Nasdaq, up a third on its $83 price; $81.3bn raised, the largest IPO ever.
4. **Senate votes down Iran war curbs** — War-powers resolution fails 51–48 as Trump says the US will "see what happens" after 1 January.
5. **Ukraine hits Crimea bridge again** — Third attack this week halts rail traffic; Russian strikes kill five and cut power in five regions.
6. **Israel pushes into central Gaza** — Tanks advance past the Yellow Line near Nuseirat in a rare ground operation despite the ceasefire.

Also today: Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast; four killed in Bangladesh unrest; Denmark backs Greenland defence deal 109–0; COP31 talks enter final stage.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` deterministically copies `entry.png` to the output directory (byte-identical on every run, no network).
